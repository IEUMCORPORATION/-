Our Services

ALL SERVICESOverview

All Services

Consulting, Settlement & Housing, Concierge, Education & Translation. We connect your Seoul headquarters with our overseas partner network to provide support from before entry through to settlement.

Consulting Service

We provide comprehensive solutions, including advisory services for global enterprises expanding into Korea, as well as partnership building and admissions processing for overseas agencies and language academies.

Key Offerings

  • B2B Global Expansion Consulting: Supporting strategic B2B partnerships with local agencies, educational institutions, and global corporate partners.
  • Korean School Admissions Consulting: Customized undergraduate and graduate admissions guidance for international students
  • Visa & Administrative Legal Support: Service support provided through our network of partnered administrative attorneys, labor attorneys, and lawyers

Settlement & Housing

We provide comprehensive settlement services for international students and global employees, including short- and long-term housing search, SIM card and bank account setup, airport greeting and pickup, and orientation programs.

Key Offerings

  • Personal Housing Search Sourcing Verified Short/Long-Term Rentals, Studios & Serviced Apartments
  • Telecom & Bank Account Setup: Foreigner SIM card activation and domestic bank account opening guidance
  • Airport Meeting/Pick-up & Life Orientation: Dedicated Incheon Airport meeting/pick-up and essential settlement support

Concierge Service

From personalized interpretation to refined lifestyle curation, we deliver bespoke concierge services tailored exclusively for you.

Key Offerings

  • Concierge Care: Instant assistance via dedicated mobile desk
  • Lifestyle Curation: Private reservations and dining curation, etc.
  • Live Interpretation Care: 1-on-1 dedicated real-time emergency interpretation, including medical accompaniment

Education & Translation

Leveraging our extensive network across Asia, we provide a full range of services—from TOPIK preparation and university admissions programs to high-quality professional translation.

Key Offerings

  • Asia Overseas Network Partnership: Recruiting prospective students through partner institutions and matching with local language academies
  • TOPIK Language Center & University Prep.: Targeted TOPIK level attainment and domestic university admission curriculum
  • Global Talent Development: Industry-academic language internship matching program
  • Professional Translation Services: Providing specialized and high-quality translation services
